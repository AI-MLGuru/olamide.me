Allowed values for each status are `pending`, `verified`, `owner_confirmed`, `blocked`, and `not_applicable`.

- **publication_permission_status:** permission to disclose names, screenshots, client work, data, architecture, and results as applicable. Owner approval of a high-level mention does not establish collaborator/client permission or approve details/assets.
- **case_study_status:** suitability and evidence for a detailed case study; this is separate from being included in the project directory.
